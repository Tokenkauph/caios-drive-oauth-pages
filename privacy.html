Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

CAIOS Google Drive Access is a personal application for the Google account owner, not a public service.

Access and use

After the owner grants permission, CAIOS uses Google's Drive API through Rclone to read and manage the owner's Google Drive files only for owner-directed automation, file organization and backups. Access includes creating, changing and deleting files when requested.

Storage and sharing

Google OAuth credentials are stored on authorized CAIOS machines to maintain access. Files may be transferred or copied to destinations that the account owner configures for particular workflows, including personal backup storage. CAIOS does not sell Google user data, display advertisements or provide Google Drive contents to outside users.

Control

The owner decides which workflows run and may stop them or revoke OAuth access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access using that authorization but does not automatically delete copies the owner previously created.

Contact

For questions, use the project repository.
