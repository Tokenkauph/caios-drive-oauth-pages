CAIOS Google Drive Access

CAIOS Google Drive Access is a personal, non-commercial tool used by the Google account owner to authorize self-hosted Rclone automation on the CAIOS Edge and Builder machines.

When authorized by the account owner, it can read, create, modify and delete Google Drive files to perform requested workflows, including file organization and backups. It is not offered to other users.

Access is authorized through Google's OAuth consent screen. The owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account connections.
